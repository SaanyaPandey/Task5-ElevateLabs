Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your detective career.

Absolutely not. AI Detective is a pure web-based experience that runs directly in your browser. No downloads, no plugins, no installations required. It works on desktop, tablet, and mobile — just open the site and start investigating.

Each mystery is crafted by experienced writers who specialize in the whodunit genre. Cases are then QA-playtested by dozens of beta detectives to ensure the clues are fair, the logic is airtight, and there are no plot holes. Every suspect needs a believable motive and opportunity.

Yes! Your solved cases, scores, and preferences are automatically saved to your browser's localStorage. This means you can close the tab and come back later without losing anything. Pro tip: Export your scores periodically if you switch browsers often.

Hard cases feature more suspects (4-6 instead of 2-3), more pieces of evidence to analyze, more questions per suspect, and deliberately ambiguous clues that point to multiple suspects at first. The culprit is designed to be the last person you'd expect — but with all the evidence pointing to them if you look carefully enough.

Every case starts with a base score of 1000 points. You earn bonus points for each clue you collect (up to 500 total) and lose points for every minute that ticks off the timer (capped at 500). The minimum score for a correct accusation is 100 points. Wrong accusations award zero — so be sure before you name the culprit!
