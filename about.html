About AI Detective

We're on a mission to bring the thrill of detective mystery gaming to everyone — beautifully designed, cleverly written, and powered by modern web technology.

OUR MISSION

Crafting the Best Detective Experience on the Web

AI Detective was born from a simple idea: the best mystery games don't need bloated installs, ad trackers, or microtransactions. They need great writing, fair clues, and satisfying deductions.

Every case we publish is written by professional whodunit authors, playtested by dozens of mystery enthusiasts, and balanced to ensure you can logically reach the solution — if you pay attention.

We believe your brain is the best gaming hardware ever built. AI Detective exists to give it a proper workout.

CORE FEATURES

Everything You Need to Solve Anything

A purpose-built toolkit designed around how real detectives think, organize, and deduce.

🧩

Logical Deduction

Every piece of evidence, every suspect statement, and every clue is logically consistent. You won't find cheap gotchas or random twists.

📊

Progress Tracking

Live progress bar shows how close you are to cracking the case based on evidence collected and questions asked. No hidden mechanics.

Smart Notebook

Full dark and light themes, tuned by designers for comfort in any lighting. Your preference persists across sessions automatically.

📱

Any Device

Perfectly responsive on desktop, tablet, and phone. Everything works offline the moment the page loads — no backend, no lag.

TECHNOLOGY

Built With Pure Web Standards

AI Detective is intentionally framework-free. No React, no Vue, no Bootstrap, no Node.js backend. This isn't a sacrifice — it's a design principle.

By building directly on top of the browser's native HTML, CSS, and JavaScript engines, we get blazingly fast load times, zero-runtime dependency weight, and the assurance that this site will run perfectly on any device for years to come.

The entire app is static files ready to deploy on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or any static host in seconds.
